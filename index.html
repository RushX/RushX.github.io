From the work

A vendor authenticated its AI integration with a static API key.

The tool had already cleared business review. I was tracing the vendor's stated controls against how the integration was actually built when the authentication method stopped making sense.

A static key means every model invocation looks identical. You can't attribute a call to a user, you can't scope what the integration reaches, and afterward there is nothing to reconstruct. The control existed on paper and produced no evidence. It went back as a blocker before purchase.
